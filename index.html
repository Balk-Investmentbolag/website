About

Patient capital, plain reporting.

Balk Investmentbolag is a simple partnership in Umeå, where a small group of co-owners invest their own capital together. We start from fundamental analysis, not short-term market moves, and ask one question of every company: would we want to own it even if the stock market closed for five years?

Each investment must pass two criteria: the quality of the business, then its price. Every decision is written down with its reasoning and what would prove it wrong, and reviewed over time.

Long-term

Holdings measured in years, not speculation.

Concentrated

A few holdings we understand well, not diversification for its own sake.

Accountable

Every decision recorded with what would prove it wrong, and reviewed.

Transparent

A full annual report to every co-owner, every year.
