Privacy

How this website handles personal data.

What the website collects

Nothing of its own. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking, and it loads nothing from other services: its typefaces, images, film and papers are all served from its own address.

The host

The website is hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages). Like any web host, GitHub records technical information when a page is requested, such as the visitor's IP address, to deliver the site and keep it secure. GitHub, which is based in the United States, handles that information under its own privacy statement.

If you e-mail us

We use your address and message only to reply to you, keep the correspondence only as long as that requires, and pass it on to no one. Our mailbox is provided by Google (Gmail).

Your rights

You can ask what personal data we hold about you, and have it corrected or erased, by writing to balkinvestmentbolag@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied with how we handle it, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY).

Who is responsible

Balk Investmentbolag, a simple partnership in Umeå, Sweden.
